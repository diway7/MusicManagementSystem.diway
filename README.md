### Welcome to Melodix!
This isn't just a coding project; it's a digital home for my favorite tracks and cozy podcasts. I wanted to build something that feels as organized and pretty as a well-curated playlist.

### Key Features 
* **`Song` Class:** For those tracks that stay on repeat 
* **`Podcast` Class:** For cozy evenings and learning new things 
* **`Artist` Class:** Supporting the creators we love 
* **`User` Class:** Because everyone needs their own musical space 

## Development Roadmap
| Week | Focus Area |
